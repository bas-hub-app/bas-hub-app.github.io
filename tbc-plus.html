Visits

A PM visit runs in five stages — Site & devices, Plan, Run, Re-test, Close — and stays in the wizard. Test on site, fix what fails on site, retest the failures, and the report carries both states: as-found and as-left. Anything still open rolls forward as a deficiency into the next visit.

Visits can be scheduled ahead, with the customer warned that testing may cause discomfort.

Testing

Devices are tested actively — batch, staggered, or snapshot — on the Siemens TBC Tool model. The test plan comes from a saved profile (the standard VAV profile ships with the module), and profiles save, so a site’s plan is built once and reused.

The proof gate

One rule governs what a test may command: a role that only reads may run on inference, but a role that commands or writes requires proof — no exceptions. A device whose type is identified with low confidence gets a safe probe, and if it still cannot be classified it is parked as unclassified, with the reason stated, and a manual-map door beside it.

Reports

The visit closes into a PM report carrying the as-found and as-left states side by side: what passed, what was deficient and what was done about it, and what was not tested — stated, never skipped silently.
