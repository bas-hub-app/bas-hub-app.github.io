How reports are built

A report starts from a named definition: the subject, the audience, the redaction rules. Reports are fed by filed records and by module output, as the catalogue below states per subject. Output is PDF, XLSX, CSV or TXT, and the preview is editable before anything is filed or sent.

Three audiences — Tech, Boss, Customer — get three registers of the same facts. Redaction is mechanical: IP addresses are masked to their first two octets, MAC addresses are masked out, and reports carry no credential fields — a credential is flagged by its existence and role only.
